The Founder / Our Mission

Meet the Founder.

Calvin Zion Brown-Fernandez
Founder & Editor, Let's Medicine

I want to become a physician not simply because medicine is intellectually challenging, but because I believe my community deserves better healthcare.

I have become increasingly concerned about what happens when patients leave medical appointments feeling unheard, when symptoms are treated without fully investigating their causes, or when a prescription becomes the answer before the right questions have been asked.

Medicine should be more than recognizing symptoms and prescribing treatments. It should be about understanding people, applying scientific evidence, investigating problems carefully, and recognizing when something doesn't make sense.

What concerns me most is that not everyone experiences healthcare equally.

Learning about racial disparities in medicine has changed how I think about the kind of physician I want to become. Black women in the United States face substantially higher maternal mortality rates than White women. Patients from historically marginalized communities also report experiences of discrimination, dismissal, and mistrust in healthcare.

Behind these statistics are real people: mothers, grandparents, children, and families who deserve to be heard and treated with dignity.

I want to be part of changing that.

As a young person of color, I understand how meaningful representation can be. I hope that one day, when someone from my community walks into an examination room and sees me, they might feel more comfortable sharing their concerns, asking questions, or seeking care they have previously avoided.

But looking like someone is not enough to earn their trust.

I want to earn that trust by becoming a physician who is knowledgeable, scientifically rigorous, compassionate, and willing to listen. Someone who looks beyond assumptions, takes symptoms seriously, explains medical decisions, and works with patients to understand the underlying causes of their health problems.

I also recognize that meaningful change cannot wait until I become a physician.

That is why I founded Let's Medicine.

I want this initiative to help make medical knowledge more accessible, encourage preventive healthcare, and create opportunities for young people and underserved communities to learn about their health.

Through education, scientific inquiry, and community service, I hope to help people feel more informed, confident, and empowered when navigating the healthcare system.

I do not believe that being young means I have nothing to contribute. I believe it means I have time to learn, opportunities to serve, and a responsibility to begin.

My goal is not just to become a doctor. My goal is to become a doctor who makes a difference — and to begin making that difference long before I earn the title.

Because Medicine Is a Human Right.

Calvin Zion Brown-Fernandez FOUNDER, LET'S MEDICINE
